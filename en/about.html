About Company

A specialist in semiconductor package process chemical materials and advanced chemical materials

ORCHEM directly develops and supplies chemical materials that are essential to the electrical and electronics industry.

Building on talented people and technical know-how, we respond quickly to a rapidly changing market and constantly challenge ourselves to develop new products and technologies. We also strive for environmentally friendly products, a safe and enjoyable workplace and transparent management.

As a company that everyone loves and employees want to come to work for, ORCHEM creates the future through challenges with a brand of its own.
