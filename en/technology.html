Core Technology

Technologies for next-generation PCB and packaging

From glass substrate plating to catalysts and via fill — ORCHEM technologies for next-generation PCB and semiconductor package processes.

Glass Core · Glass Interposer

TGV Fill Plating

Zenith-1000 · Zenith-1500

We developed both the electrolytic copper plating that fills through glass vias (TGV) with copper and the adhesion promoter that forms the bonding layer between glass and ABF. Our own electroless chemicals are used for the conductive seed step.

  • Adhesion promoter molecular structure designed and synthesized in-house (multi-functional group)
  • Adhesion measured by peel test after thermal shock (288 ℃, solder floating 10 s, 10 cycles)

Electroless Copper Plating

Long-Life Catalyst

ORC-445 series

We improved catalyst life and bath stability for electroless copper plating. In an 80 ℃ high-temperature storage test, conventional catalysts precipitated on day 2, while our new catalyst showed no abnormality after 30 days.

  • Long life: 4+ weeks of use
  • No discoloration, sediment or particle size change after 30 days
  • Multi-ligand design reduces foreign matter in ionic PTH processes
  • Coverage for various materials including T-Glass and E-Glass

Electrolytic Copper Plating

Via Fill Plating

Zenith series

ORCHEM was the first in Korea to develop a PCB via fill product and supplies it to domestic customers. Our line-up covers standard, pattern, high-speed and pulse plating to suit each process.

  • Through Hole T/P > 90 %, Dimple < 5 ㎛ (Zenith-800)
  • Flat pattern shape (Zenith-850)
  • Stronger suppression with high-adsorption aromatic additives (Zenith-910)
  • Easy performance control with a three-component system
